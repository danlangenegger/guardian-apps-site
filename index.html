Backlog Guardian for Jira

Keep Jira backlogs healthy

Backlog Guardian automatically identifies common backlog hygiene issues and summarizes them in a simple Backlog Health Score. Teams can quickly see where attention is needed and drill down into the affected Jira issues.

Automated checks Detect stale issues, unassigned work, overdue items, missing estimates and stale epics.
Backlog Health Score Understand overall backlog health with a clear score from 0 to 100 and a transparent penalty breakdown.
Issue drill-down Review affected Jira issues and act directly on the underlying work.
